# Governance

kuberoot began as one person's attempt to find out whether Kubernetes could become an operating system rather than something installed on top of one. This page describes how it is run today, and how that will change as others join.

## Where we are

For now, the project has a single maintainer, its founder Timur Tukaev ([@tym83](https://github.com/tym83)), who decides what belongs in it, what is merged, when releases ship and who else becomes a maintainer. This is a starting point, not a destination: the aim is to share the project with the people who build it.

## How decisions are made

Decisions are made in the open, on GitHub; if it is not written down there, it has not been decided. Anyone may propose a change. A pull request is merged once a maintainer approves it and continuous integration passes, and whoever made a declined proposal is told why.

## Roles

**Contributors** are everyone who takes part, in code, recipes, documentation, reviews or testing; no permission is needed. **Reviewers** are contributors whose judgement in an area the maintainers have come to rely on. **Maintainers** merge changes, cut releases and share in decisions; they are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Sharing responsibility

Responsibility follows sustained contribution: work carried on over months, changes of substance merged in an area, careful reviews of others' work there, and conduct in keeping with the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Anyone may ask, or be nominated by a maintainer; the maintainers decide and record it in MAINTAINERS.md.

Once the project has three maintainers or more, its decisions pass from the founder to the maintainers together: by lazy consensus in the open, and by a simple majority vote where consensus cannot be reached. This page will be revised then.

A maintainer may step down at any time, and one long absent may be made emeritus, with our thanks. Changes to this page are made by pull request and decided by the maintainers.
